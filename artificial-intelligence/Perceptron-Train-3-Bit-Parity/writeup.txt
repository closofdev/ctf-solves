  (2,-2,-2,1) (2,-2,2,0) (2,2,-2,0) (2,2,2,1)

maxSteps 16, lr 0.02-20.0, threshold 0.75, init w=[1,1,1] b=0 acc 0.25.
Parity tak linearly separable oleh satu bidang; 75% (6/8) maksimum.
